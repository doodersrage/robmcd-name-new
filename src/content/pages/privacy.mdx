Last updated: September 6, 2026

Robmcd.name (the "Site") is operated by Robert McDowell. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

**Contact form**

If you use the [contact form](/contact), we collect your name, email address, and message so we can reply. Messages are sent by email to the Site operator and are not used for marketing lists or sold to third parties.

**Cloudflare Turnstile**

The contact form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which helps block automated abuse. Turnstile may process technical signals (such as browser and request characteristics) under Cloudflare's privacy terms. We receive a verification result, not a marketing profile.

**Email**

If you email [admin@robmcd.name](mailto:admin@robmcd.name) directly, normal email metadata and content apply as with any mailbox.

**Logs and hosting**

The Site runs on Cloudflare. Like most sites, request logs may include IP address, user agent, referring URL, and pages visited, used for security, debugging, and reliability. Optional Cloudflare Web Analytics (if enabled) is privacy-oriented and does not use cross-site advertising cookies.

**Theme preference**

A theme choice (light/dark) may be stored in your browser's `localStorage` so the Site can remember it. That value stays on your device.

**Cookies**

We do not set advertising cookies. Cloudflare or Turnstile may set cookies required for security or delivery. You can refuse cookies in your browser; some features (such as the contact form challenge) may not work if you do.

**Third parties we do not use for ads**

We do not use Google Analytics or similar ad-tech trackers on this Site.

**Security**

We take reasonable steps to protect information in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on mail infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

**Changes**

We may update this policy by posting a new version here with a revised date. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

**Contact**

Questions about privacy: use the [contact form](/contact) or email [admin@robmcd.name](mailto:admin@robmcd.name).
