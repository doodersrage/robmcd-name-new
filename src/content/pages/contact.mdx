Tell me what you are stuck on and what “done” looks like. Infrastructure, legacy stack repair, servers and databases, or questions about [LLM Prompt Studio](/llm-prompt-studio) and [ThermalTrace](/work/thermaltrace) — all fine.

1. You describe the problem and constraints.
2. I clarify scope — often audit → stabilize → then modernize if it still makes sense.
3. We agree on a first step before any large rewrite.

This form is the only contact path on the site (spam control). I read every submission and usually reply within a couple of days.
